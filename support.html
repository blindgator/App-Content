Frequently asked questions

How do I buzz in during a Versus match?

Once the clue has finished being read, do a one-finger double tap anywhere on the screen. The whole screen acts as the buzzer. Be quick, because your opponents are trying to beat you to it.

Does the app work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Every screen was designed and tested with VoiceOver, and the game is fully playable without sight.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account and no sign-up, and the game works offline. Your stats and leaderboard are stored on your device.

How do I reset my stats?

Open Stats from the main menu and choose Erase All Stats and Leaderboards.

What happened to my win streak?

Winning a Versus match makes you champion and carries your winnings into the next day. Losing a match ends the streak and starts you over.
