That Real Blind Tech Show — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Overview

That Real Blind Tech Show is a free iOS app for listening to the That Real Blind Tech Show podcast and reading related articles and announcements. It is built to respect your privacy completely. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, no login, and no registration. It never asks for your name, email, or any personal detail. It uses no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

To function, the app saves some information locally on your device only, and never sends it anywhere: your episode playback positions, any episodes you download for offline listening, a cached copy of the episode list, and your notification preferences. This stays on your device, and you can remove all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Network connections

To show the latest episodes and articles, the app retrieves publicly available content over the internet — the podcast's public RSS feed and a public content file listing articles and announcements. These requests deliver content to your device and carry no personal information. As with any internet request, the servers hosting that public content may record standard technical details such as an IP address under their own privacy policies; we do not receive or have access to that information.

Notifications

The app can notify you about new episodes and announcements. These are generated on your device — there is no notification server, and no information about you leaves your device to deliver them. You can control notifications in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share, rent, or sell any information, because we collect none.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles information ever changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to ThatRealBlindTechShow@gmail.com.
